Let your AI ring your phone.

Add Twring to Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app that lets you add a custom connector in its settings. When it needs you, it can ring your phone or send you a message in Twring, and read your reply. AI apps can ring you and message you, but you can't hold a live voice call with them; for that, connect Claude Code, Codex or Hermes on your computer.

https://api.twring.com/mcp

What it can do

  • Ring your phone with a short reason (at most 140 characters) when it needs you. The ring is full screen, even on a locked phone. When you answer, Twring reads the reason aloud, then opens that AI's chat so you can reply by voice or text.
  • Send you a message, with up to five files by link, without ringing. Good for results and anything that can wait.
  • Read your replies in that chat the next time it checks.
  • Check whether a ring can get through right now, for example if you have Do Not Disturb or quiet hours on.

The ring goes over the internet to the Twring app, so no phone number or telephony account is involved.

Add it

First, get an invite, install Twring on your iPhone or Android phone, and sign in. Then:

  1. Claude. Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector. Paste the address above, then sign in and allow.
  2. ChatGPT. Settings, Apps and Connectors (or Developer mode, Create). Paste the address above, then sign in and allow.
  3. Grok Bot, Cursor and other apps. Look for Connectors in the app's settings, add a custom connector with the address above, then sign in and allow.

Sign in the same way you sign in to the Twring app (Google, or Apple), with the same account. Rings go to the account, not to whichever phone is in your hand, so if the connector uses a different account than your phone, the ring has nowhere to go.

Asking it to ring you

Once connected, say what you want in plain words. For example:

  • "Research these three suppliers, and ring me when you have a recommendation."
  • "Draft the reply, send it to my Twring chat, and only ring me if you need a decision."
  • "If anything in this report looks wrong, ring me."

The AI reads your answer the next time it checks for replies, so it may not react the very second you answer. A good habit for it is to message by default and ring only when you must know or decide now. If it rings too often, tell it so in the chat.

What it can and can't see

A connected AI app gets only what you write or say in its own Twring chat. It can't read your other chats, your agents or your calls. You can remove it anytime in Twring: Settings, Connected AI apps.

What it doesn't do

You can't place a live voice call to Claude or ChatGPT from Twring: chat apps don't take incoming calls. The connector is for the AI reaching you, and for you replying in its chat. For live two-way calls, connect an agent you run yourself, such as Claude Code, Codex or Hermes. Those can ring you too, and you can call them back: how each agent rings you.

What's a connector?

A connector is how you add tools to an AI app. Most use MCP, the standard way AI apps add tools. Twring's connector is an MCP server at one address, https://api.twring.com/mcp, and it's listed in the official MCP Registry.

Other ways to get a ring or a ping

  • A real phone call over the phone network. Telephony companies offer their own MCP servers. As of November 2026, Plivo's MCP docs describe one for its voice and messaging services. That route uses a telephony account and phone numbers, which makes sense if you need to call ordinary phone numbers.
  • A push notification. If a buzz is enough and you never need to talk back, a simple notification service may be all you need.

Twring fits when you want a ring you can't miss, a reply channel in the same place, and no phone number or carrier setup.

When it doesn't ring

  • Check the account. The connector and the phone app must be signed in to the same Twring account.
  • Check Twring's settings. Do Not Disturb and quiet hours in Twring stop rings. Notifications must be on for Twring.
  • Check it's still connected. Look in Settings, Connected AI apps. If it isn't listed, add the connector again.

Questions

Can I call Claude from Twring?

Not the Claude or ChatGPT app itself: AI chat apps don't take incoming calls. Your connected AI can ring you, and you can reply in its Twring chat. For live calls, connect an agent you run, such as Claude Code, Codex or Hermes.

What do I hear when it rings?

Twring reads the reason it gave, then opens its chat so you can answer by voice or text. It reads your answer the next time it checks.

Does my phone need a phone number for this?

No. The ring reaches the Twring app over the internet, so there is no phone number or telephony account involved.

Which AI apps can use the Twring connector?

Claude, ChatGPT, and other apps that let you add a custom connector in their settings, such as Grok Bot and Cursor. They all use the same address, https://api.twring.com/mcp.

Why didn't my phone ring?

Most often the connector was signed in with a different account than your phone, or Do Not Disturb or quiet hours are on in Twring. Rings go to the account, not the device.

How do I remove it?

In Twring: Settings, Connected AI apps, Remove. It can't reach you again unless you allow it.

What data does it get?

Only what you write or say in its own Twring chat. Not your other chats, agents or calls. See the privacy notice.
