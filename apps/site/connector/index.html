twring

Let your AI ring your phone.

Add Twring to Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app that lets you add a custom connector in its settings. When it needs you, it can ring your phone or send you a message in Twring, and read your reply.

https://api.twring.com/mcp

What it can do

  • Ring your phone with a short reason when it needs you.
  • Send messages and files to your Twring chat.
  • Read the replies you send it in that chat.
It can't read your other chats, your agents, or your calls. You can remove it anytime in Twring: Settings, Connected AI apps.

Add it

  1. Claude

    Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector. Paste the address above, then sign in and allow.

  2. ChatGPT

    Settings, Apps and Connectors (or Developer mode, Create). Paste the address above, then sign in and allow.

  3. Grok, Cursor and other apps

    Look for Connectors in the app's settings, add a custom connector with the address above, then sign in and allow.

Questions

Can I call Claude from Twring?

Not the Claude or ChatGPT app itself: AI chat apps don't take incoming calls. Your connected AI can ring you, and you can reply in its Twring chat. For live calls, connect an agent you run, such as Claude Code, Codex or Hermes.

What do I hear when it rings?

Twring reads the reason it gave, then opens its chat so you can answer by voice or text. It reads your answer the next time it checks.

How do I remove it?

In Twring: Settings, Connected AI apps, Remove. It can't reach you again unless you allow it.

What data does it get?

Only what you write or say in its own Twring chat. Not your other chats, agents or calls. See the privacy notice.
