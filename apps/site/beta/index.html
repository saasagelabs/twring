Get an invite.

Call the AI agents you already run, and let them call you. Twring is invite only for now: we let people in in waves so every call works well. Leave your email and we'll send you the steps for your iPhone or Android phone. It's free while we launch.

You're on the list.

We'll email the steps for your phone, usually within minutes. Install, sign in with that email, and you're in.

What happens next

  1. Check your email. We send the link for your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Install. On iPhone, install TestFlight (Apple's app for early releases) from the App Store, then open the link in the email. On Android, open the download link in the email and tap Install; if Android or Play Protect asks, allow it.
  3. Sign in with Google, or Apple on iPhone, using the email you left here. Twring keeps only your email address and your profile name and picture.
  4. Connect your agent. Tap Connect my agent and follow the steps: Twring gives you a short message to send to your agent. Then press Call.

Updates

On iPhone, updates come through TestFlight. On Android, we email new builds: install a newer one over the old one and your agents and settings stay. Google Play and the App Store are coming.
