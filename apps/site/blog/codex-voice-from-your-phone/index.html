How to talk to Codex by voice from your phone

There are two ways, as of November 2026: OpenAI's ChatGPT Voice works with Codex on iOS once you pair your phone with the ChatGPT desktop app, and Twring lets you call the Codex CLI already running on your computer, from an iPhone or Android phone. Pick OpenAI's if you use the ChatGPT desktop app; pick Twring if you live in the Codex CLI, want Android, or want Codex to ring you when a job is done.

Option one: OpenAI's own voice for Codex

As of November 2026, OpenAI's docs describe two phone-related features:

  • Codex in the ChatGPT mobile app. You pair your phone with a Mac or Windows PC by setting up remote access in the ChatGPT desktop app and scanning a QR code. Then you can start or continue tasks on that computer, or in the cloud, approve requested actions, and review changes. A connected computer must stay awake and online. (OpenAI docs.)
  • ChatGPT Voice. A live spoken conversation that replies out loud, handles interruptions, and can start tasks, check progress and send follow-ups. OpenAI lists it in the ChatGPT desktop app and in Codex on iOS after pairing. The page doesn't list the Codex CLI or the IDE extension, and availability depends on your plan, rollout and workspace. (OpenAI docs.)

If you're on iPhone, use the ChatGPT desktop app, and have access, that's the most direct route, and you can approve actions from the phone.

Option two: call the Codex CLI with Twring

Twring is a phone app that calls the Codex you already run in your terminal. It works on iPhone and Android. You press Call, speak, and hear Codex's answer. Codex keeps its own model, instructions and tools, and the usage counts on your own account, as if you had typed.

  1. Get an invite at twring.com/beta and install Twring: on iPhone through TestFlight, on Android as an APK from twring.com (not Google Play).
  2. In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server.
  3. Either paste the short message it gives you into Codex and approve the command Codex runs to connect itself, or tap Scan a code on my computer, run the command it shows in your project folder, and scan the code it prints.
  4. Both ways download the Twring connector from twring.com. It needs Node.js 20 or newer (Node.js is the free program that runs the small Twring connector).
  5. Codex appears in the app. Press Call.

Full setup is also on the Codex page.

What a Codex call sounds like

Your words reach Codex as a message, in the folder you connected. One thing to know: Codex sends its answer whole, not word by word, so Twring starts speaking when the answer is complete. On a longer task Twring may say a short line so the call isn't silent, then the answer.

You can talk over Codex at any time and it stops speaking; that's called barge-in. Calls and typed chats with Codex sit side by side in the app, and every call continues the same Twring conversation.

Read-only, unless you let it write

On a call, Codex works in a read-only sandbox: it can read your files but not change them. To let it write in that folder, add --codex-sandbox workspace-write to the connect command.

Twring never runs Codex's tools. It only carries your words in and the answer out. Whatever Codex does stays under Codex's own sandbox and settings.

Let Codex ring you

In a Twring call or chat, Codex is told how to ring your phone. Ask "ring me when the build finishes" and it rings full screen, even on a locked phone, with a short reason. If you hang up while it's still working, it keeps going, and its answer lands in your Twring chat.

You can also make Codex ring you after every turn it finishes, using Codex's own notify setting. In the connector's folder, run node twring-connect.mjs hooks install codex --on-turn-complete. Because that fires after every turn, it's off unless you ask for it. Add --message to get a chat message instead of a ring.

Which should you pick?

  • ChatGPT Voice if you use the ChatGPT desktop app, you're on iOS, you have it in your plan, and you want to approve Codex's actions from your phone.
  • Twring if you run the Codex CLI, you're on Android, or you want Codex to ring you like a phone call. You can also call other agents, like Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Hermes, from the same app.
  • Neither if you only need to glance at progress: OpenAI's mobile app without voice may be enough.

What Twring needs

  • An invite (Twring is free while it launches).
  • Codex signed in on your computer, and Node.js 20 or newer.
  • The computer on and awake, with the connector running. If it sleeps, Codex shows as offline.

Twring listens only during a call and never records or keeps your audio (privacy notice). If Codex doesn't answer, check the computer first, then see support.

Questions

Why does Codex pause before answering on a call?

Codex reports its answer when it is finished rather than word by word, so Twring can only start speaking once the whole answer is ready. Short questions come back quickly; long tasks take as long as they take in the terminal.

Can I approve Codex's actions by voice with Twring?

Twring runs Codex in a read-only sandbox by default, or a workspace-write sandbox if you choose, so it does not stop to ask. To approve individual actions from your phone, OpenAI's Codex in the ChatGPT mobile app supports that.

Does Twring work with Codex on Android?

Yes. Twring runs on iPhone and Android and calls the Codex CLI on your computer, so the phone you use doesn't matter.
