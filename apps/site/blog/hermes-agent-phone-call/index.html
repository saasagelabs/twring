Call your Hermes agent from your phone, and let its scheduled jobs ring you

Install the Twring plugin in Hermes (hermes plugins install twring, then hermes twring link and hermes gateway), and your own Hermes appears in the Twring app on your iPhone or Android phone: press Call and talk to it live. Deliver any Hermes scheduled job to twring, and your phone rings with the first sentence of its result.

What you get

Twring is a phone app for the AI agents you already run. The Hermes plugin makes Twring a Hermes messaging platform, a bit like Telegram, except you can also talk. Three things change once it is set up:

  • Live calls. Press Call in the app and speak. Your words reach Hermes as a message, and Twring speaks its answer as it streams in.
  • Approvals by voice or tap. When Hermes needs your OK for a risky command, the request shows up on your phone.
  • Rings from scheduled jobs. A job that delivers to twring puts its result in your Twring chat and rings your phone.

Hermes keeps its own model, memory, tools and permissions. Twring only handles the voice: it turns your speech into text for Hermes, and Hermes's reply into speech for you. Twring never runs Hermes's tools.

Set it up

  1. Get an invite and install Twring on your iPhone (through TestFlight) or Android phone (an APK from twring.com). Sign in.
  2. On the computer or server where Hermes runs, install and link the plugin: 
    hermes plugins install twring
hermes twring link
  3. In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server, Scan a code on my computer, and scan the code the link step shows.
  4. Start the Hermes gateway so your phone can reach Hermes: 
    hermes gateway
  5. Hermes appears in the app. Press Call.

hermes gateway setup offers the same link step. hermes twring status shows whether you are linked, and hermes twring unlink forgets the link on that computer (delete Hermes in the app too).

What a call with Hermes is like

You talk, Hermes answers out loud as its reply arrives. A few things make it feel like a call rather than swapping voice notes:

  • You can talk over it. Start speaking and Hermes's voice stops at once, while its work carries on. If you go on to ask something new, Twring replaces the old turn and Hermes stops that run, as if you had typed /stop, then answers what you just said.
  • Hanging up does not stop the work. If you hang up while Hermes is still busy, it keeps going, and the rest of its answer reaches your Twring chat, with a ring if your Twring settings allow.
  • One conversation. Typed messages in the Twring app and spoken turns on a call reach the same Hermes conversation, so you can start by voice and carry on by text.

Approving risky commands from your phone

When Hermes wants to run a command that needs your OK, the approval request appears on your phone. On a call, Twring reads a short fixed line saying Hermes wants to run a command, and asks you to say yes or no, or tap. The details sit on the card on your screen.

Your answer goes back through Hermes's own approval system. The plugin never approves anything for you, and silence means no. If a command is too long to show on the phone, you get a card that can only deny it from the phone; approving that one has to happen at the computer.

We cover this in more depth, agent by agent, in approving your agent's risky actions by voice.

Make a scheduled job ring you

Hermes can run jobs on a schedule (cron jobs: tasks that run at set times). Point any of them at twring as the place to deliver results. When the job finishes:

  1. Its result is saved in your Twring chat with Hermes.
  2. Your phone rings, full screen even when it is locked, and Twring reads the first sentence of the result (at most 140 characters).
  3. Answer to hear it, or read the full result in the chat later.

Prefer a quiet notification to a ring? Set TWRING_RING_ON_DELIVERY=false in Hermes's settings. Write the job so its first sentence stands alone, for example "The nightly backup finished and all checks passed." That sentence is what you hear.

How it compares with Hermes's own voice features

Hermes has its own voice mode. As of November 2026, its docs describe push-to-talk in the Hermes command line, spoken replies in Telegram and Discord chats, and a bot that joins Discord voice channels, with a choice of speech providers, some of which run locally.

If you mostly talk to Hermes at your desk, or you already live in Discord, its built-in voice may be all you need. Twring is for a different moment: you are away from the computer, you want a phone call with your own Hermes, and you want Hermes to be able to ring you when something is done or needs a decision.

When it doesn't work

  • Hermes doesn't appear in the app. Check that hermes gateway is running. Your phone reaches Hermes only while it runs.
  • Two "Hermes" agents in the app. You may also be running Twring's separate connector program for Hermes on the same computer. Stop it and use only the plugin, or the two take the same link from each other.
  • No ring from a scheduled job. Check the job delivers to twring, that TWRING_RING_ON_DELIVERY is not set to false, and that Do Not Disturb and quiet hours in Twring's settings aren't on.
  • You run your own Twring server. Link with hermes twring link --server https://your-twring-server. See self-hosting.

Still stuck? The support page has more, and the full plugin page is at twring.com/hermes.

What the plugin sends, and what it doesn't

The plugin keeps one outbound connection to your Twring server while the gateway runs. It sends Hermes's replies, approval questions and scheduled-job results, plus the name of each tool Hermes starts and whether it succeeded (never the tool's arguments or output). It writes no files of its own, runs no shell commands and sends no telemetry. Twring never records or keeps call audio; the privacy notice lists what it does keep.

Questions

Do I need a phone number or a Twilio account?

No. Twring calls happen over the internet inside the Twring app, so there is no phone number or telephony account to set up.

Can a scheduled Hermes job send a notification instead of a ring?

Yes. Set TWRING_RING_ON_DELIVERY=false and results still land in your Twring chat, with a notification instead of a ring.

What happens if I hang up while Hermes is working?

Hermes keeps working. The rest of its answer reaches your Twring chat, with a ring if your Twring settings allow.

Is the plugin open source?

Yes. It is licensed Apache-2.0 and lives in the Twring repository on GitHub, along with the rest of Twring.
