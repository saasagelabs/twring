How to talk to Gemini CLI with your voice

Gemini CLI does not include voice input or spoken replies (its README doesn't mention any, as of November 2026), but you can call it with Twring: a phone app that sends your speech to the Gemini CLI on your computer and speaks its answers back. If you want Google's own voice agent instead, Antigravity has a voice mode in its app and CLI.

Does Gemini CLI have a voice mode?

Not as of November 2026. The Gemini CLI README doesn't mention voice input, dictation or speech output. Gemini CLI is open source (Apache 2.0), so you can always add your own tooling, but there's nothing built in.

Google's separate agent product, Antigravity, does have one. Its voice mode is a two-way spoken conversation powered by Gemini Live, in the Antigravity app and the Antigravity CLI (1.5.0 or later), and it answers out loud. If you're open to switching tools, that's Google's own route. If you want to keep using Gemini CLI, read on.

How to call Gemini CLI with Twring

  1. Make sure Gemini CLI is installed and signed in: run it once in a terminal.
  2. Get an invite at twring.com/beta and install Twring: on iPhone through TestFlight, on Android as an APK from twring.com.
  3. In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server.
  4. Either paste the short message it gives you into Gemini CLI and approve the command Gemini CLI runs to connect itself, or tap Scan a code on my computer, run the command it shows in your project folder, and scan the code it prints.
  5. Both ways download the Twring connector from twring.com. It needs Node.js 20 or newer (Node.js is the free program that runs the small Twring connector).
  6. Gemini CLI appears in the app. Press Call.

You can also do this from your desk: the call is on your phone, but you can put it on speaker or Bluetooth.

What happens on a call

Your words reach Gemini CLI as a message, in the folder you connected, with its own model, memory and tools. Twring speaks the answer as it streams in. Talk over it at any time and it stops speaking; that's called barge-in, and it's what makes it feel like a call. Say "stop" to cancel the current turn.

Calls and typed chats with Gemini CLI sit side by side in the app. Usage counts on your own Google account, as if you had typed.

Under the hood, Twring talks to Gemini CLI through ACP, the Agent Client Protocol (a standard way for apps to drive coding agents). You don't need to set that up; the connector does it.

You choose what it may use

Gemini CLI asks before it uses a tool. On a call, Twring says no to every such request unless you allowed that kind of tool when you connected. That keeps a voice call from quietly changing things you can't see.

To let it read and search your files, add --acp-allow read,search to the connect command. Start there. Twring never runs Gemini CLI's tools itself; it only passes along the permission choices you set up front.

The practical upshot: with no flags, Gemini CLI on a call can talk with you from what it already knows and what you say, but not look at your project. Add read and search, and it can answer questions about your code.

Let Gemini CLI ring you

In a Twring call or chat, Gemini CLI is told how to ring your phone. Ask it to call you when a long job is done, and it rings full screen, even on a locked phone, with a short reason. If you hang up while it's still working, it keeps going, and its answer lands in your Twring chat.

Other ways to speak to Gemini CLI

  • Your computer's own dictation. macOS, Windows and most phones have built-in dictation that can type into any terminal. It's free and already there, but it's one-way: you still read the answers.
  • A voice tool for MCP-capable agents. Open-source projects like VoiceMode add voice conversations on your computer, using its microphone and speakers. VoiceMode is built for Claude Code and says it supports other MCP-capable agents (MCP is the standard way AI apps add tools). Check its docs for Gemini CLI before relying on it.
  • Twring when you want to talk from your phone, away from the computer, and get spoken answers.

What you need

  • An invite. Twring is free while it launches.
  • Gemini CLI signed in, and Node.js 20 or newer on the computer.
  • The computer on and awake with the connector running. If it sleeps, Gemini CLI shows as offline in the app.

Twring never records or keeps your audio (privacy notice). The short version of setup is on the Gemini CLI page, and support covers what to do if a call doesn't connect.

Questions

Why does Gemini CLI say it can't read my files on a call?

By default Twring denies every tool request on a call. Connect again with --acp-allow read,search added to the command, and Gemini CLI can read and search the folder you connected.

Do I need a Google AI subscription for Twring?

Twring itself is free while it launches. Gemini CLI uses whatever sign-in it already has on your computer, so its usage counts on your own Google account, as if you had typed.

Can I use Twring with Antigravity instead?

Twring's connector works with Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Hermes, or any command you name. Antigravity has its own voice mode, which is the simpler choice if you use Antigravity.
