Twring compared

Twring is a phone app that lets you call the AI agent you already run and talk to it live, and lets your agent ring your phone. Other tools do some of this differently, and some are a better fit for some jobs. These pages say which, fairly, with the date we checked.

Comparisons

  • Twring vs Claude Code Remote Control. Remote Control is for typing to and steering your Claude Code session from the Claude app. Twring is for talking to it and getting rung.
  • Claude Code voice mode alternatives. Claude Code has dictation, not a voice that talks back. The options that do talk back: VoiceMode at your computer, Twring on your phone.
  • Let Claude Code call you, with or without Twilio. call-me and similar plugins, agentring, ntfy and Twring.
  • Every way to reach your coding agent from your phone. Typing, dictating, talking and getting rung, across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and more.

What Twring is not

  • Not a phone number, SMS or carrier service.
  • Not a builder for business voice agents or call centres.
  • Not an AI model: your agent keeps its own.
  • Not a remote terminal: you talk to your agent, and it works where it already runs.

Questions

Is Twring a phone-number or SMS service?

No. Rings and calls go over the internet to the Twring app, so there is no phone number, Twilio account or carrier to set up.

Is Twring a voice agent builder like Vapi, Retell or Bland?

No. Those build business voice agents for phone lines. Twring gives a voice to an agent you already run, such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Hermes.

Is Twring a new AI model?

No. Your agent keeps its own model, memory and tools. Twring turns your speech into text for it, and its reply into speech for you.
