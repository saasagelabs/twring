Why didn't my phone ring?

Start in the Twring app: open Settings and look at Calls. It says Ready when your agents can ring this phone, even when it's locked. If it says Not ready yet, fix the items it lists. If it's ready and rings still don't come, work through the checks below in order.

1. The phone

  • Notifications for Twring. If they're off, rings can't reach you. Settings, Calls asks for permission or opens your phone's settings.
  • On Android: the "Incoming agent calls" notifications. If that kind of Twring notification is muted, turn it back on. Settings, Calls opens the right page.
  • On Android: full-screen notifications. They let a ring show over the lock screen. Settings, Calls shows a fix if they're off.
  • Your phone's own Do Not Disturb or Focus. These can silence rings, like other calls.

2. Twring's settings

  • Do not disturb in Twring stops every ring. You can still call your agents.
  • Quiet hours stop rings during the hours you set.
  • When work finishes after a call: if this says Send a message, work that finishes after you hang up arrives as a message, not a ring. Set it to Ring me if you want the ring.

3. The account

Rings go to your Twring account, not to whichever phone is in your hand. Sign in on your phone with the same account you connected the agent to. For Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app, the account you signed in with when you added Twring to it must be the same one, too.

4. Limits

  • Each agent can ring you at most 6 times an hour.
  • Only one ring at a time can be waiting for you.
  • A ring can't reach you while you're already on a Twring call.

These hold back bursts of rings on purpose.

5. The agent's side

  • An agent on your computer rings through the Twring connector, so the computer must be on and online, with the connector running. The agent's page in the app shows whether it's online.
  • Rings by themselves need a hook. For Claude Code run node twring-connect.mjs hooks install claude; for Codex, node twring-connect.mjs hooks install codex --on-turn-complete. Run it in the folder where you downloaded twring-connect.mjs. A hook installed with --message sends a message instead of ringing.
  • Hermes rings for scheduled jobs delivered to twring, unless TWRING_RING_ON_DELIVERY is set to false. See Hermes.

Missed it?

A ring lasts 30 seconds. After that, the app shows a missed call with the reason your agent gave, so you can call it back.

Still stuck? Write to support@twring.com with the time it should have rung and which agent it was. A person reads every email. How rings work explains the rest.

Questions

Why didn't my phone ring when my agent called?

Most often notifications are off for Twring, Do Not Disturb or quiet hours are on in Twring, or the agent is connected to a different Twring account than the one on your phone. In Twring, Settings, Calls says Ready when this phone can ring.

Why did the first ring work but the next ones didn't?

Each agent can ring you at most 6 times an hour, and only one ring can be waiting at a time. A ring also can't reach you while you're already on a Twring call.

Does a ring work when my phone is locked?

Yes. It fills the screen on a locked iPhone or Android phone. On Android, full-screen notifications must be allowed for Twring; Settings, Calls shows a fix if they are off.

What happens to a ring I miss?

A ring lasts 30 seconds. After that, the app shows a missed call with the reason your agent gave.
