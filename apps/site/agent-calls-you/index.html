Let your AI agent call you

Your agent can ring your phone like a real call, full screen even when it's locked, when a job is done or it needs your OK. Answer and you're talking to it. No phone number or phone plan is needed: the ring comes through the Twring app.

What a ring looks like

  • Full screen, even on a locked phone. You see which agent is calling and the short reason it gave, for example "The migration finished and all tests pass."
  • Answer and talk. For an agent on your computer, you're straight into a live call. It speaks first, tells you why it rang, and carries on in the same session it was working in.
  • Or decline with a reply. Busy, Outside, Let's text, or up to 60 characters of your own. Your agent gets the reply.
  • Missed it? A ring lasts 30 seconds. After that, the app shows a missed call with the reason.

Set it up once

  1. Get an invite and install Twring on your iPhone (through TestFlight) or Android phone (an APK from twring.com).
  2. In the app, tap Connect Agent and follow the steps for your agent: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, or an AI app like Claude or ChatGPT.
  3. Turn on notifications for Twring on your phone. Rings can't reach you without them.

How each agent rings you

Claude Code and Codex: ask them

In a Twring call or chat, these agents are told how to ring your phone. So just ask: "Run the full test suite and ring me when it's done." It rings with a short reason when it's finished. Hang up mid-task and it keeps going, and its answer lands in your Twring chat.

Claude Code: rings by itself when it's waiting on you

Add a Twring hook to Claude Code once, and it rings you whenever Claude Code sends one of its notifications, mostly when it needs permission or is waiting for your input. On the computer, in the folder where you downloaded Twring's twring-connect.mjs, run:

node twring-connect.mjs hooks install claude

The reason on your phone is Claude Code's own message. The Claude Code ring guide covers the details and what happens to the permission prompt.

Codex: a ring after every turn, if you want it

Codex can ring you each time it finishes a turn, with the start of its reply as the reason. It's off until you turn it on:

node twring-connect.mjs hooks install codex --on-turn-complete

That can be a lot of rings, so many people add --message to get a message in their Twring chat instead.

Hermes: scheduled jobs ring you

With the Twring plugin for Hermes, deliver any scheduled Hermes job to twring. Its result is saved in your Twring chat, and your phone rings with its first sentence. Set TWRING_RING_ON_DELIVERY=false if you'd rather get a notification.

Claude, ChatGPT and other AI apps

Add Twring to your AI app, and it can ring you when it needs you. AI chat apps can't take a live call, so when you answer, Twring reads out the reason it gave and opens its chat. Reply by voice or text, and it reads your reply the next time it checks.

Keeping rings useful, not noisy

  • Quiet hours and Do Not Disturb. In Twring's settings, pick hours when no agent can ring you. You can still call your agents any time.
  • Limits on bursts. Each agent can ring you at most 6 times an hour, one ring at a time, and never while you're already on a call.
  • Messages for small news. Add --message to a hook if you'd rather read updates later.

A ring or a notification?

For news you can read later, a notification is enough. If you only want Claude Code pings, Anthropic's Remote Control can send push notifications to the Claude app, and a hook can post to a notification service such as ntfy. A ring is for the moments you don't want to miss, and you can answer it and talk to the agent that rang. Every way to reach your coding agent from your phone compares the options.

When a ring doesn't arrive

  • Check that notifications are on for Twring, and that Do Not Disturb and Twring's quiet hours are off.
  • Sign in to Twring on your phone with the same account you connected the agent to. Rings go to that account.
  • For an agent on your computer, check that the computer is on and online. The agent's page in the app shows whether it's online. Answering also needs it online, and uses your daily calling time.
  • If you set up a hook in a different folder than twring-connect.mjs, run the install command again from that folder.

Questions

Do I need a phone number or a Twilio account?

No. Your agent rings you through the Twring app, so there's no phone number to rent, no phone plan and no per-minute phone bill.

Will it ring on a locked phone?

Yes. On iPhone and Android the ring fills the screen, even when the phone is locked, as long as notifications are on for Twring.

Which agents can ring me?

Claude Code and Codex ring you when you ask them to in a Twring call or chat. Claude Code and Codex can also ring by themselves, through a hook you add once. Hermes rings you when a scheduled job delivers to Twring. Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot and other AI apps can ring you once you add Twring to them.

Can I approve a permission request by answering?

It depends on the agent. Hermes sends its approval requests to your phone, and you answer by voice or tap. A Claude Code prompt stays in your terminal: the ring tells you it's waiting, and you can talk it through on the call. Twring never approves or runs an agent's tools itself.

How often can an agent ring me?

At most 6 times an hour for each agent, one ring at a time. It can't ring you while you're on a call, or during your quiet hours.

Is it free?

Yes, while we launch. Answering a ring uses your daily calling time, like any call. Pricing has the details.
