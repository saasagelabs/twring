Talk to Codex from your phone.

Twring calls the Codex you already run on your computer. Press Call, say what you need, and hear its answer as it comes. When a job is done or it needs your OK, it can ring your phone.

Get an invite

Set it up

  1. Get an invite and install Twring on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server. Then either:
    • paste the short message it gives you into Codex, and approve the command Codex runs to connect itself, or
    • tap Scan a code on my computer, run the command it shows in your project folder, and scan the code it prints.
    Both download the Twring connector from twring.com. It needs Node.js 20 or newer.
  3. Codex appears in the app. Press Call.

What happens on a call

  • Your words reach Codex as a message, in that folder, with its own model, instructions and tools.
  • Twring speaks the answer as it streams in. Talk over it at any time and it stops speaking.
  • Calls and typed chats with Codex sit side by side in the app.

Read-only, unless you let it write

On a call, Codex works in a read-only sandbox: it can read your files but not change them. To let it write in that folder, add --codex-sandbox workspace-write to the command.

Let it ring you

In a Twring call or chat, Codex is told how to ring your phone. Ask it to call you when a long job is done, and it rings you with a short reason. If you hang up while it's still working, it keeps going, and its answer lands in your Twring chat.

Twring also works with Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Hermes, and any agent you can reach at its own web address. AI apps like Claude and ChatGPT can ring and message you too.

Questions

Does Twring replace Codex's model?

No. Twring only handles the voice: it turns your speech into text for Codex, and its reply into speech for you. Codex does the thinking with its own model and tools. Twring never runs its tools.

Does it use my own account?

Yes. Twring runs the Codex that is signed in on your computer, so its usage counts on your own account, as if you had typed.

Are my calls recorded?

No. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. The privacy notice lists everything it does keep.

Is Twring free?

Free while we launch. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK.
