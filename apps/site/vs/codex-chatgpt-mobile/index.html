Twring vs Codex in the ChatGPT app

OpenAI's ChatGPT app lets you start Codex tasks from your phone, send instructions, approve commands and review changes. Twring is a phone app for talking to the Codex CLI on your computer in a live call, and for letting it ring you. If you want to type, approve and read diffs on an iPhone, use OpenAI's app first.

What the ChatGPT app does well

  • It's official. Made by OpenAI, in the app you may already use.
  • Approve and review. Check requested commands before Codex continues, and read diffs and test results on your phone.
  • Cloud or your computer. Run tasks in Codex Cloud, so your computer doesn't need to be on, or on a connected Mac or Windows PC.
  • Voice on iPhone. With a paid plan, ChatGPT Voice can start Codex tasks, check progress and change direction.

What Twring does differently

  • Android too. Twring runs on iPhone and Android, and calls the Codex CLI on your computer, so the phone doesn't matter.
  • A ring, not a banner. Ask Codex to "ring me when the build finishes", or turn on a ring after every turn it finishes.
  • The CLI you already run. Codex works in the folder you connected, with its own settings, and usage counts on your own account.
  • Other agents in the same app. Call Claude Code and Hermes too.

Choose the ChatGPT app if

  • You want to type to Codex, approve its commands and read its diffs on an iPhone.
  • You run tasks in Codex Cloud.

Choose Twring if

  • You use Android, or you live in the Codex CLI.
  • You'd rather talk than type, and want Codex to ring you like a phone call.

Questions

Can I use Codex from my phone without Twring?

Yes. OpenAI's ChatGPT app for iOS lets you start Codex tasks, send instructions, approve commands and review changes, running in Codex Cloud or on a connected Mac or Windows PC.

Does Codex have voice on my phone?

ChatGPT Voice can start and steer Codex tasks in Codex on iOS after you pair your phone with a desktop host, on Plus, Pro, Business, Edu and Enterprise plans, according to OpenAI as of 26 November 2026.

Does Twring work with Codex on Android?

Yes. Twring runs on iPhone and Android and calls the Codex CLI on your computer, so the phone you use doesn't matter.

Can I approve Codex's commands with Twring?

No. Twring runs Codex in a read-only sandbox by default, or workspace-write if you choose, so it doesn't stop to ask. To approve individual commands from your phone, use OpenAI's ChatGPT app.
