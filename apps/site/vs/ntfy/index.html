Twring vs ntfy

ntfy is a free, open source way to send push notifications to your phone, and a common choice for Claude Code hooks. Twring rings your phone full screen when your agent needs you, and lets you answer and talk to it. If a banner is all you need, ntfy is simpler.

What ntfy does well

  • Simple and general. Send a notification from anything with one web request. Not just agents: builds, servers, scripts.
  • Free and self-hostable. Use ntfy.sh for free or run your own server.
  • Quiet. A banner doesn't interrupt you the way a ring does.

What Twring does differently

  • A ring you won't miss. Full screen, even on a locked phone, with the reason your agent gave.
  • You can answer. For an agent on your computer, answering puts you straight into a live call. It tells you why it rang and carries on in the same session.
  • Your agent can call itself. Ask "ring me when the tests finish" in a call or chat, and it does. How rings work.
  • Limits on purpose. Each agent can ring you at most 6 times an hour, and quiet hours stop rings at night.

Choose ntfy if

  • A notification is enough, and you'll read it when you get to it.
  • You want one tool for alerts from many scripts and servers.

Choose Twring if

  • You'd miss a banner, and want a ring when your agent is stuck or done.
  • You want to answer and talk it through, not just read a line.

Plenty of people use both: ntfy for routine alerts, Twring for the moments that need you. For Claude Code, see make Claude Code ring your phone when it needs you.

Questions

Can ntfy call my phone?

On paid ntfy.sh plans, ntfy can call a verified phone number and read the message aloud, in English. It's one way: you can't talk back.

Is a notification enough?

Often, yes. If you'll see a banner in time, ntfy or your agent's own notifications are simpler. A ring is for the moments you can't miss, and lets you talk to your agent.

Can I use ntfy with Claude Code?

Yes. Community recipes add a Claude Code hook that sends a notification to your ntfy topic when Claude Code needs you or finishes.

Does Twring need a phone number?

No. Twring rings its own app over the internet, so there is no phone number or phone plan to set up.
