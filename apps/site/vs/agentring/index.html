Twring vs agentring

Both let your AI agent ring your phone over the internet, with no phone number or Twilio account, and both are open source. The big difference is who talks on the call. In agentring, a realtime voice AI talks with you and passes the conversation to your agent. In Twring, you talk to your agent itself: Twring turns your speech into text for it and reads its own reply aloud.

What agentring does well

  • In the app stores. Install from Google Play or the App Store, sign in and scan a code.
  • Free without limits on your own server. Its self-hosted setup has no caps, and you pick the voice AI and key.
  • The call keeps going while your agent works. The voice AI can talk with you while an agent is slow, and the answer arrives when it's ready, or as a message if you've hung up.
  • Many agents through one tool set. Anything that speaks MCP can message you, wait for your reply or call you.

What Twring does differently

  • You talk to your agent, not to a second AI. Your words reach your agent as a message, and what you hear is its own reply. No separate voice model decides what you meant.
  • Twring never runs your agent's tools. Your agent keeps its own permissions and decides what it does. agentring's own README advises giving its voice AI only the tools and agents you'd trust someone holding your phone with.
  • AI apps ring you too. Add Twring to Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app as a custom connector, and it can ring and message you with nothing to install on your computer.
  • No recordings. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. How Twring keeps calls and keys safe.

Choose agentring if

  • You want a voice assistant that talks for your agents and keeps the call going while they work.
  • You want to install from an app store today.
  • You want to run everything yourself, free and without limits.

Choose Twring if

  • You want to hear your agent's own answers, and cut in while it talks.
  • You don't want a second AI deciding what to tell your agent.
  • You also want Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app to ring you.

Questions

Does agentring work on iPhone?

Yes. As of 26 November 2026, agentring's README lists an App Store app for iOS 17 or newer as well as its Android app. With your own server, it says an iPhone gets calls and messages only while the app is open.

Do I need a phone number for either?

No. Both ring an app on your phone over the internet, so there is no phone number, Twilio account or per-minute phone bill.

Who speaks on the call?

In agentring, a realtime speech model talks with you and hands the transcript to your agent. In Twring, your agent's own reply is read aloud, and your words reach your agent as text.

Which is free?

Both, with limits on the hosted versions. agentring's free hosted plan has 10 call minutes a month; on your own server it's free without limits. Twring is free while it launches, with a daily amount of calling time and up to 6 rings an hour per agent.

Can I run either on my own server?

Yes. agentring needs a server or computer with Docker, a Firebase project and a voice AI key. Twring installs with one command on a Linux machine with Docker and a Deepgram or xAI key.
