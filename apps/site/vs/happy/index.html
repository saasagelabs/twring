Twring vs Happy

Happy is a free, open source app for following your coding agents from your phone: you type to them, approve their requests, review their changes and can start a voice call. Twring is built around the call: you talk to your agent, and it rings your phone full screen when it needs you. If you mostly type and review code, Happy does more.

What Happy does well

  • Everything you'd do at the keyboard. Type to your agents, approve or deny their requests, review changes as colored diffs and attach screenshots.
  • Many agents and accounts. Switch models, reasoning effort and accounts in the middle of a chat.
  • Teams. A shared server with chats, a feed of what shipped and single sign-on.
  • In the app stores, and open source. Install from the App Store or Google Play; the code is MIT licensed.

What Twring does differently

  • Your agent rings you. When a job is done or needs your OK, your phone rings full screen, even when it's locked, and you can answer or decline with a short reply. How rings work.
  • Calls are the main thing. What you hear is your agent's own reply, read aloud as it streams, and you can talk over it to stop it. Calling time is a daily amount while Twring is free, not a monthly allowance.
  • AI apps ring you too. Add Twring to Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app as a custom connector, and it can ring and message you.
  • Your own server, documented. One command runs Twring on your own Linux server next to your agents.

Choose Happy if

  • You mostly type to your agents, approve their requests and review their changes.
  • You work with a team and want a shared view of what your agents did.
  • You want to install from an app store today.

Choose Twring if

  • You'd rather talk to your agent, hands free, than type.
  • You want your phone to ring when your agent needs you, not just a banner.
  • You use Hermes, or want Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app to ring you.

Questions

Does Happy have voice?

Yes. As of 28 November 2026, Happy's App Store page describes real-time voice calls with your agents, with 20 free minutes every 30 days, through its voice provider ElevenLabs. You can also send messages by voice.

Can Happy ring my phone?

Happy's App Store page describes notifications when an agent needs you or finishes. We found no mention of it ringing your phone like a call. Twring rings full screen, even when your phone is locked.

Is Happy free?

Happy is free to use, with in-app purchases. You bring your own access to the agents, such as a Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan or an API key. Twring is free while it launches, with a daily amount of calling time.

Which agents does each work with?

Happy lists Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Antigravity and more. Twring works with Claude Code, Codex, Hermes and agents at their own web address, and AI apps such as Claude and ChatGPT can ring and message you.

Do I need a phone number for either?

No. Both work through an app on your phone over the internet, so there is no phone number or phone service account to set up.
