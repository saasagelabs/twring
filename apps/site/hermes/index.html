Call your Hermes. Hermes calls you.

Twring is in the Hermes plugin catalog. Install it, and you can talk to your own Hermes in a live call from your phone, answer its approval questions by voice, and get a ring when a scheduled job is done.

Get an invite

Set it up

  1. Get an invite and install Twring on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. On the computer or server where Hermes runs:
    hermes plugins install twring
hermes twring link
    In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server, Scan a code on my computer, and scan the code it shows.
  3. Start the Hermes gateway, so your phone can reach Hermes:
    hermes gateway
    Hermes appears in the app. Press Call.

What happens on a call

  • Your words reach Hermes as a message, with its own model, memory, tools and permissions. Twring speaks its answer as it streams in.
  • Talk over Hermes at any time: its voice stops at once. Ask something new and it stops the old run and answers you. Hang up and it keeps working.
  • Typed messages and spoken turns reach the same Hermes conversation.

Approvals by voice or tap

When Hermes needs your OK for a risky command, the request appears on your phone. Say yes or no, or tap. The answer goes through Hermes's own approval system, and silence means no.

It rings you

Deliver any Hermes scheduled job to twring. Its result lands in your Twring chat, and your phone rings with its first sentence. Set TWRING_RING_ON_DELIVERY=false to get a notification instead of a ring.

Hang up while Hermes is still working, and the rest of its answer reaches your chat, with a ring if your Twring settings allow. How rings work covers quiet hours, limits and declining with a reply.

Handy commands

  • hermes twring status shows whether this computer is linked.
  • hermes twring unlink forgets the link on this computer. Then delete Hermes in the app too.
  • hermes twring link --server https://your-twring-server links to your own Twring server instead.

Already run Hermes through Twring's computer program, twring-connect.mjs? Stop it before linking the plugin. Otherwise the app shows two "Hermes" agents.

The Hermes guide walks through setup and scheduled jobs in more detail, and the plugin's README lists exactly what it sends to Twring.

Twring also works with Claude Code and Codex, and any agent you can reach at its own web address. AI apps like Claude and ChatGPT can ring and message you too.

Questions

Does Twring replace Hermes's model?

No. Twring only handles the voice: it turns your speech into text for Hermes, and its reply into speech for you. Hermes keeps its own model, memory, tools and permissions.

Can Twring approve commands for me?

No. Only you can approve, by voice or tap, through Hermes's own approval system. If you don't answer, the answer is no.

Are my calls recorded?

No. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. The privacy notice lists everything it does keep.

Is Twring free?

Free while we launch. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK. Pricing has the details.
