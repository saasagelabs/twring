Pricing

Twring is free while we launch. There's no paid plan today, and nothing is ever charged without your OK.

What's free today

  • The Twring app on iPhone and Android, by invite.
  • Live calls with your agents: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, or an agent at its own web address.
  • Rings: your agent calls your phone, full screen even when it's locked, when a job is done or needs your OK.
  • Chats with each agent, with voice notes and files both ways.
  • Letting Claude, ChatGPT or another AI app ring and message you.

There's no phone number to rent and no per-minute phone bill: rings come through the Twring app.

Limits while it's free

  • Calling time: each person has a daily amount of calling time. It starts again every day at midnight UTC.
  • A busy day: if Twring's calling time for everyone runs out, new calls wait until midnight UTC. Calls already going keep going.
  • Rings: your agent can ring you a few times an hour at most, so a burst of rings is held back on purpose.
  • Access: by invite for now. Ask for one here.

What you still pay others for

  • Your agent. Claude Code, Codex and Hermes keep running on your own account with the company that makes them, so whatever you pay for your agent today stays the same. Twring doesn't run your agent's model or pay for it.
  • Your own server, if you run one. If you run Twring on your own server, you pay for the server and for your own speech account (Deepgram or xAI).

Running Twring on your own server

Self-hosting is free while we launch. It will have a yearly charge, paid in the app, for the ring service Twring runs so that locked phones can ring. Before any charge starts, you'll be told in the app and by email, with the price. Why self-hosting will have a yearly charge.

If prices change

If Twring ever has a price, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK. Questions: support@twring.com.

Questions

Is Twring free?

Yes, while we launch. There's no paid plan today. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK.

Do I need a Twilio account or a phone number?

No. Your agent rings you through the Twring app, so there's no phone number to rent and no per-minute phone bill.

Is there a limit on calls?

Yes. While Twring is free, each person has a daily amount of calling time, and it starts again at midnight UTC. Your agent can also ring you only a few times an hour.

Does Twring pay for my agent?

No. Your agent keeps running on your own account with the company that makes it, so whatever you pay for it today stays the same. Twring handles the calls and chats.

What does running my own Twring server cost?

It's free while we launch. It will have a yearly charge for the ring service that makes locked phones ring, and you'll be told the price in the app and by email before it starts. You also pay for your own server and speech account.
