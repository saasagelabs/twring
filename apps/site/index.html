A ring from an AI app (Claude, ChatGPT and Grok Bot) is different: Twring reads the reason aloud, then opens the app's chat so you can reply by voice or text. There is no live call.

Your agent calls when it matters.

No need to keep checking terminals and chat threads. Your agent rings your phone when a job is done or when it needs your OK.

  • Rings like a real call

    Full screen, even when your phone is locked. The ring shows why your agent is calling.

  • Two kinds of ring

    Answer Claude Code, Codex or Hermes and you are in a live call. Answer an AI app (Claude, ChatGPT and Grok Bot) and Twring reads its reason aloud, then opens its chat for your reply.

  • Decline with a reply

    Choose Busy, Outside or Let's text, or type up to 60 characters. Your agent receives your reply.

  • Quiet hours

    Set the hours when no agent can ring you. You can still call your agents at any time.

What you ask for on a call gets done.

A spoken request is easy to lose once the call ends. Twring gives your agent a short guide that tells it to keep a written list.

  1. It confirms the request

    One short line on the call, so you know it heard you.

  2. It writes the request down

    Your words and the time go into a list kept where your agent runs. Passwords and keys are never written down.

  3. It checks the list after every call

    It works through the open items, oldest first, without a reminder.

  4. It reports back in your chat

    Each result goes to your agent's Twring chat, and the item is marked done with a note of where the result is. If it cannot do something, it tells you why.

If your agent cannot write anything down, it says so on the call and asks you to type the request in its chat, where it is kept. The guide is added for Hermes, Claude Code and Codex when your agent links your computer. Any other agent can use it: download the guide.

Questions

What is Twring?

A phone app for iPhone and Android that lets you call the AI agent you already run, such as Claude Code, Codex, Hermes or OpenClaw, and talk to it live. Your agent can also ring your phone, full screen even when it's locked, when a job is done or needs your OK. No phone number is needed. Twring only handles the voice: your agent keeps its own model, memory and tools. It's free while we launch, open source, and you can run it on your own server.

Which agents work with Twring?

There are two groups. Talk live: agents you run yourself on your computer or server, like Hermes, Claude Code and Codex, and agents you can reach at their own web address. You call them and talk in a live call, and they can ring you. Rings and messages: AI apps (Claude, ChatGPT and Grok Bot) connect online. They can message you and ring you, but they do not take live calls: connect an AI app.

How do I install it?

Ask for an invite and we email you the steps.

  1. On iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the link in the email.
  2. On Android, Twring installs as an APK from twring.com, not from Google Play: open the download link in the email and tap Install. If Android or Play Protect asks, allow it.
  3. Sign in with Google, or Apple on iPhone, using the email you gave us. Then connect your agent and press Call.
Does Twring record my calls?

No. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. The privacy notice lists everything it does keep.

Is it free?

Free while we launch. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK. Pricing has the details.

Can I run Twring on my own server?

Yes. One command sets up Twring on your own Linux server, next to your agents, with your own speech account. Your chats and files stay there, and so do your calls unless your server uses Twring's audio service. Self-hosting explains how, and why it will have a yearly charge (free while we launch).
