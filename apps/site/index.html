When it matters, your agent calls.

Stop checking terminals and chat threads. Your agent rings you the moment a job is done or it needs your OK.

  • Rings like a real call

    Full screen, even on a locked phone. Answer and you're straight into a conversation, with the reason it called.

  • Decline with a reply

    Busy, Outside, Let's text, or type up to 60 characters. Your agent gets the message.

  • Quiet hours

    Pick the hours when no agent can ring you. You can still call them any time.

Ask on a call. It gets done.

A request made out loud is easy to lose when the call ends. So Twring gives your agent a short guide, and the guide makes it keep a written list.

  1. It says what it will do

    One short line on the call, so you know it heard you.

  2. It writes it down, right then

    Your words and the time, in a list kept where your agent runs. Never a password or a key.

  3. It reads the list after every call

    And works through what's open, oldest first, without being reminded.

  4. It reports back in your chat

    Each result goes to its Twring chat, and the item is marked done with where the result is. If it can't do something, it tells you why.

If your agent can't write anything down, it says so on the call and asks you to type the request in its chat, where it stays. The guide is added for Hermes, Claude Code and Codex when your agent links your computer. Any other agent can use it: download the guide.

Questions

What is Twring?

A phone app that lets you call the AI agents you already use, and lets them ring you. It gives your agent a live voice without replacing its brain, tools or memory.

Which agents work with Twring?

Agents you run yourself on your computer or server, like Hermes, Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI. Agents you can reach at their own web address connect online. AI apps like Claude can also message you and ring you: connect an AI app.

How do I install it?

Ask for an invite and we email you the steps.

  1. On iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the link in the email.
  2. On Android, open the download link in the email and tap Install. If Android or Play Protect asks, allow it.
  3. Sign in with Google, or Apple on iPhone, using the email you gave us. Then connect your agent and press Call.

Google Play and the App Store are coming.

Does Twring record my calls?

No. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. The privacy notice lists everything it does keep.

Is it free?

Free while we launch. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK.

Can I run Twring on my own server?

Yes. One command sets up Twring on your own Linux server, next to your agents, with your own speech account. Your chats and files stay there, and so do your calls unless your server uses Twring's audio service. Self-hosting explains how, and why it will have a yearly charge (free while we launch).

Why an invite?
