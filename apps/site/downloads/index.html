Downloads

Everything Twring offers to download, in one place. You rarely need these by hand: the app sets things up for you.

The Twring app

For iPhone and Android. When you get an invite, we email you the download link. On iPhone it comes through TestFlight, Apple's app for early releases. On Android it's an app file from twring.com, not from Google Play.

Twring on your computer

Lets an agent on your computer, such as Claude Code, Codex or Hermes, take your calls and ring you. In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server, and it shows you what to run. It needs Node.js 20 or newer.

The file it downloads: twring-connect.mjs. Setup guides: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes.

The guide for your agent

A short guide that teaches your agent how to talk on a call, when to ring you, and to write down what you ask for. It's added for you when you link a computer. To add it to another agent by hand, unpack it into that agent's skills folder.

twring-skill.tar.gz

Claude, ChatGPT and other AI apps

Nothing to download. Add Twring in the app's settings so it can ring your phone: see how.

Your own Twring server

One command sets it up on a Linux machine, so your chats, files and agents stay on your server. Run Twring on your own server.
