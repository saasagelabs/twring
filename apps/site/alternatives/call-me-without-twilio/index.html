Let Claude Code call your phone, with or without Twilio

Popular plugins like call-me let Claude Code phone you over the phone network, which needs a Twilio or Telnyx account, a phone number and a public web address. If you'd rather skip all that, an app that rings over the internet, like Twring or agentring, needs no phone number or carrier account.

Calling you over the phone network

These open source projects place a real phone call to your number. They're a good fit if you already have a carrier account, or want a call that reaches any phone without an app.

  • call-me (MIT): a Claude Code plugin that calls you on the phone. It needs Telnyx or Twilio, an OpenAI key for speech (or local speech), and a free ngrok account so the carrier can reach your computer. Its README estimates about $0.03 to $0.04 a minute with OpenAI speech, plus around $1 a month for a number.
  • awesome-claude-call (MIT): calls through a Twilio number you own, with OpenAI's realtime voice by default, and opens a Cloudflare tunnel for you instead of ngrok.
  • AgentComms (MIT): phone calls through Twilio and ngrok, plus messages on Discord, Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp and other chat apps.
  • Better Call Claude (MIT): calls, SMS and WhatsApp both ways, tested with Twilio.

For scale: as of October 2026, Twilio's US voice pricing was $0.0140 a minute for an outbound call and $1.15 a month for a local number.

Ringing an app, with no phone number

  • Twring (open source): a phone app for iPhone and Android. Claude Code, Codex, Hermes and AI apps can ring it full screen, even on a locked phone. Answer and you're in a live call with that agent: your words go to it as text, and its own reply is spoken back. Free while it launches, by invite.
  • agentring (Apache-2.0): rings its own app (Android and iPhone) over the internet, with no phone number or Twilio. A realtime speech model does the talking and passes the conversation to your agent. Hosted at agentring.io with a free plan, or on your own server. Twring vs agentring.

Just a notification

  • Remote Control can push a notification to the Claude app when a task finishes or needs a decision. See Twring vs Remote Control.
  • ntfy (open source) sends push notifications from a Claude Code hook to its phone app, free at ntfy.sh or on your own server. On its paid ntfy.sh plans it can also phone a verified number and read the message aloud, one way.

Which to pick

  • You want a call to any phone, and don't mind a carrier account: call-me or one of its cousins.
  • A banner is enough: Remote Control's pushes, or ntfy.
  • You want a ring you can't miss, no phone number, on iPhone or Android, and to talk to the agent that rang: Twring. Set up the Claude Code ring.

Questions

Can Claude Code call my phone without Twilio?

Yes. Twring and agentring ring an app on your phone over the internet, so there is no phone number or carrier account. Both have apps for iPhone and Android.

When is a real phone call better?

When you want to reach a regular phone number without installing an app, or you already pay for a carrier account. Plugins like call-me, AgentComms and Better Call Claude do that.

What does a phone-network call cost?

It depends on the carrier. As of October 2026, Twilio's US price for an outbound call was $0.0140 a minute, plus a monthly number. The call-me README estimates about $0.03 to $0.04 a minute in total with OpenAI speech.

Is a notification enough?

Often, yes. Remote Control can push a notification to the Claude app, and ntfy can push to your phone from a Claude Code hook. A ring is for the moments you must not miss, and lets you talk back.
