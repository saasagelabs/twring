About Twring

Twring gives the AI agents you already run a voice. You call your agent from your phone and talk to it live, and it can ring you when a job is done or needs your OK.

Who makes it

Twring is built by Rish and run under the name SaaSage Labs, from India. Say hi on X: @rishflips, or write to hello@twring.com.

What Twring is built on

Bring any agent brain. Get the voice.

  • Voice is input and output, not intelligence. Your agent keeps its own model, memory, tools and permissions. Twring turns your speech into text for your agent, and its reply into speech for you.
  • Twring never runs your agent's tools. Your agent does its own work, where it already runs.
  • It should feel like a call. Speech streams both ways, and you can interrupt at any moment.
  • No call recordings. Twring doesn't record or keep your call audio. The privacy notice lists what it does keep.

Open source

The source is public on GitHub. The server, relay and self-host kit are AGPL-3.0; the app, the computer tool and everything else are Apache-2.0. You can also run Twring on your own server.

Where else you'll find Twring

  • github.com/saasagelabs/twring: the source code.
  • The official MCP Registry, as com.twring/twring: the connector for AI apps.
  • The Hermes plugin catalog: the Twring plugin for Hermes.
  • Glama: the connector's listing.

Built with

LiveKit carries the live audio. Deepgram, Cartesia and xAI turn speech into text and text into speech.

Where Twring is today

On Android (from twring.com, not yet on Google Play) and iPhone (through TestFlight, not yet on the App Store). It's by invite, and free while we launch.

Help with the app: support@twring.com. Everything else: hello@twring.com. A person reads every email.
