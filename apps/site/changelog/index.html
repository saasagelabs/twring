What's new in Twring

What changed in Twring, a week at a time, newest first. Dates are when a change was finished; the app update reaches phones in the following days. It starts in November 2026; every change is also on GitHub.

Week of 23 November 2026

  • Claude Code rings you only when it needs you: the Claude Code hook now rings for permission prompts and idle waits, not every notification.
  • "Ring me when it's done" on the screen after a call ends.
  • A quicker first call: a one-page welcome and a Call now button.
  • Fewer interruptions: Twring says "one moment" only when your agent takes a long time, and says so if an answer breaks off.
  • Clearer speech: spoken replies skip tables, file paths and long codes, and say when the code is on screen instead.
  • Steadier calls: the sound stays on the speaker or headset you picked, Bluetooth is used only for calls, and a regular phone call pauses Twring.
  • Your calling time: the app says when today's calling time is nearly used up.
  • Help when your computer is offline: the app explains how to bring an agent on your computer back.
  • Chats: search your chats, reply to a message, copy, share or delete one, export a chat as Markdown, and see every photo and file per agent. Drafts and unsent messages survive a restart.
  • Listen: scrub and change the speed when a reply is read aloud.
  • Private notifications: message previews are sealed for your phone, so Google's and Apple's push services pass them on without being able to read them.
  • Chats reconnect at once when you come back to the app or the network returns.
  • Self-hosted server 1.0.5: release notes.

Week of 16 November 2026

  • The language you speak: set it in Settings, and Twring hears you and picks voices for it. Twring's own lines are in 10 languages.
  • A new layout: agents and chats in one list, three tabs, and a portrait call screen with a large End button.
  • Recents: delete one call or clear the whole list.
  • Voice drops: an answer cut off when the voice service drops carries on after the reconnect.

Week of 9 November 2026

  • Hermes plugin: Twring became a Hermes messaging platform, so you can call your Hermes agent and approve its commands by voice. Twring for Hermes.
  • Talk while it answers: what you say while your agent's answer plays is sent to it, and an answer you talked over still plays before the next one.
  • Long code in chats wraps instead of showing an empty bubble.

Week of 2 November 2026

  • Grok voices: add your own xAI key to use Grok voices on your calls.

Questions

How do I get the latest version?

On iPhone, TestFlight installs updates for you. On Android, download the newest app from the downloads page; the app tells you when an update is needed.

Where is the full history?

Every change is a pull request in the Twring repository on GitHub. Versions of the self-hosted server and the Hermes plugin are listed under Releases.
