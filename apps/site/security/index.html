Security at Twring

Twring handles the voice between you and your agent, and nothing more. It doesn't record your calls, it stores keys and chats encrypted, and it never runs your agent's tools: your agent keeps its own permissions and decides what it does. The privacy notice lists everything Twring keeps.

Your calls

  • No recordings. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. It keeps no transcript of what was said on a call either: a call shows in your chat as a card with its time, length and outcome.
  • Speech services. Call audio goes to a speech service (Deepgram) to become text, and your agent's reply goes to it to become speech. If you add your own xAI key, xAI does this for your calls only.
  • No double sends. If the network drops, Twring doesn't re-send your words to your agent, so a risky action is never run twice because of a reconnect.

Your agent

  • Twring never runs your agent's tools. Your words reach your agent as text, the same as if you had typed. Your agent keeps its own model, memory, tools and permissions.
  • Careful defaults on your computer. On a call, Claude Code can read your files but not change them, unless you add --claude-allow-edits. Codex runs in a read-only sandbox unless you add --codex-sandbox workspace-write.
  • Nothing secret written down. The short guide Twring gives your agent tells it to write down what you ask for on a call, but never passwords or keys.

Your keys and data

  • Keys are encrypted. An agent's key, and any speech account key you add, is encrypted on the Twring server (AES-256-GCM), never shown again and never logged.
  • Chats and files are encrypted. Messages and files are stored encrypted on the Twring server, and only your account can read them. Photos have their location data removed on your phone before they're sent.
  • Only you. Your agents, chats and calls are tied to your account, and only you can see and call your agents.
  • Sign-in. You sign in with Google, or Apple on iPhone. Twring never sees your password.
  • Logs leave secrets out. Keys, tokens and web address query strings are removed from logs and crash reports.
  • Delete any time. Delete my data in the app's Settings removes your data.

Open source, and your own server

The source is public on GitHub: the app and the computer tool are Apache-2.0, and the server, relay and self-host kit are AGPL-3.0. If you'd rather keep chats, files and agents on your own machine, run Twring on your own server.

Report a security problem

Email support@twring.com with "Security" in the subject, and please don't open a public issue. Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what it affects. We reply within 2 working days, keep you posted until it's fixed, and credit you once a fix is out if you'd like.

Questions

Does Twring record my calls?

No. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio or a transcript of the call.

Can Twring run commands on my computer?

No. Twring never runs your agent's tools. Your words reach your agent as text, and your agent decides what to do under its own permissions.

How are my keys stored?

Encrypted on the Twring server with AES-256-GCM. They're never shown again and never written to logs.

How do I delete my data?

In Twring, open Settings and tap Delete my data.

How do I report a security problem?

Email support@twring.com with "Security" in the subject. We reply within 2 working days.
