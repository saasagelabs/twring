Talk to Gemini CLI from your phone.

Twring calls the Gemini CLI you already run on your computer. Press Call, say what you need, and hear its answer as it comes.

Get an invite

Set it up

  1. Get an invite and install Twring on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. In the app, tap Connect Agent, On my computer or server. Then either:
    • paste the short message it gives you into Gemini CLI, and approve the command Gemini CLI runs to connect itself, or
    • tap Scan a code on my computer, run the command it shows in your project folder, and scan the code it prints.
    Both download the Twring connector from twring.com. It needs Node.js 20 or newer.
  3. Gemini CLI appears in the app. Press Call.

What happens on a call

  • Your words reach Gemini CLI as a message, in that folder, with its own model, memory and tools.
  • Twring speaks the answer as it streams in. Talk over it at any time and it stops speaking.
  • Calls and typed chats with Gemini CLI sit side by side in the app.

You choose what it may use

Gemini CLI asks before it uses a tool. On a call, Twring says no to every request unless you allowed that kind of tool when you connected. To let it read and search, add --acp-allow read,search to the command.

Let it ring you

In a Twring call or chat, Gemini CLI is told how to ring your phone. Ask it to call you when a long job is done, and it rings you with a short reason. If you hang up while it's still working, it keeps going, and its answer lands in your Twring chat.

Twring also works with Claude Code, Codex and Hermes, and any agent you can reach at its own web address. AI apps like Claude and ChatGPT can ring and message you too.

Questions

Does Twring replace Gemini's model?

No. Twring only handles the voice: it turns your speech into text for Gemini CLI, and its reply into speech for you. Gemini CLI does the thinking with its own model and tools. Twring never runs its tools.

Does it use my own Google account?

Yes. Twring runs the Gemini CLI that is signed in on your computer, so its usage counts on your own account, as if you had typed.

Are my calls recorded?

No. Twring listens only while a call is on, and never records or keeps your audio. The privacy notice lists everything it does keep.

Is Twring free?

Free while we launch. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first, and nothing is charged without your OK.
