# Security

Please report security problems privately to **support@twring.com** with "Security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it affects. We reply within 2 working days and keep you posted until it is fixed. We credit reporters who want it once a fix is out.

Supported: the latest release of the app, the hosted service at twring.com, and the latest self-host image.
